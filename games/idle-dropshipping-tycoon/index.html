Idle Dropshipping Tycoon

Get it on Google Play

About the game

A business simulation game for every would-be entrepreneur: grow from a bedroom side hustle into a global e-commerce empire, even offline.

In Idle Dropshipping Tycoon you run your own virtual online store: read the trends, find the right niche product, pick your supplier, spend your marketing budget wisely, and make every call yourself, from carrier choice to refunds. A dropshipping simulator at heart, with the relaxed pace of an idle tycoon game.

💤 EARN WHILE YOU'RE AWAY

Your store keeps selling when you close the game: come back to offline earnings at half your recent profit rate, for up to 8 hours, or up to 16 hours with the Night Shift upgrade. Once a day, you can watch a rewarded video to double them.

📺 NO FORCED ADS

There is not a single forced ad in the game. Ads are rewarded videos you watch only when you want to. Purchases are optional: the whole game can be finished without buying anything.

🛍️ 77 NICHE PRODUCTS, 7 CATEGORIES

Wireless earbuds, aromatherapy candles, smart pet feeders: enter a market while the trend is rising, set your price while competition drops. Trends and competition shift all the time, so your shop never runs the same way twice.

📈 YOUR MARKETING, YOUR RULES

Social media, search engine or influencers? Each channel converts differently. Shift your budget to the right channel and your margins multiply. Launch the campaign and let the orders come knocking.

🚚 SHIPPING, SUPPLIERS, RATINGS: REAL BUSINESS DETAIL

Three carriers to ship your orders, three suppliers, delivery times, refund rates, customer reviews. Express shipping keeps customers happy and raises your store rating, and a higher rating brings in more organic orders. The right supplier cuts refunds; a slow, sloppy supply chain costs you reputation.

🏆 GROW, SELL, RESTART

Level up, unlock products and stores, go global at Tier 4. Then sell the company: the bigger the run, the more investor points. Spend them on lasting upgrades in the Investor Board, earn stars on your products, and open the European, Asian and American markets in big runs.

⚔️ TAKE OVER YOUR RIVALS

Buy shares in rival companies: every stake pays dividends, 60% gives you control, and at 100% the company is yours; each step unlocks a perk.

🔬 RESEARCH, WEEKEND EVENTS, SEASON PASS

Unlock permanent upgrades in the Research Lab. Every weekend from level 5, race rival shops in Campaign Week with themed products and win trophies: each one adds +1% income for good, up to +10%. The Season Pass runs four weeks and turns your daily play into rewards. Collect 10 experts, each with a permanent bonus.

🌍 IN YOUR LANGUAGE

English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese and Korean. Built for short sessions: at work, on the go, whenever you have a minute. The game works offline too; leaderboards, rewarded videos and purchases need an internet connection.

🥇 LEADERBOARDS

Race every player under a name you choose: total investor points, weekly product stars and Campaign Week points. No account, no sign-in.

Start your entrepreneur story now: your first order arrives within minutes.
