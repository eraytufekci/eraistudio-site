Idle Tok: Influencer Tycoon

Get it on Google Play

About the game

Go from bedroom vlogger to star streamer in Idle Tok: Influencer Tycoon, an influencer game you can play offline! Start with a tiny channel, collect fans, views and subscribers, and build a global social media empire. No forced ads: rewarded ads only, and only when you want them.

Idle Tok is a vertical idle tycoon game set in the world of short videos: a streamer simulator and content creator game where your channel keeps producing while you are offline. Make videos, collect views and likes, put your cash into a better camera, microphone and lighting, and hire managers to handle the channel for you.

HOW TO PLAY

  • MAKE VIDEOS: Tap to record a video. Better gear brings more views and more fans per video.
  • UPGRADE: Camera, microphone, lighting and editing levels raise your view rate and like ratio. Buy ×1, ×10, ×100 or MAX.
  • HIRE MANAGERS: Upload, filming and trend managers do the work; you plan the next move.
  • LIVESTREAM: Short livestreams reward fast combos with gifts from your fans, just like a real streamer. The faster you go, the bigger the gifts.
  • REBRAND: With enough followers, rebrand your channel, gain permanent Hype, move into a bigger studio and level up faster every time.
  • PICK A NICHE: General, dance, comedy, gaming or food, each with its own multipliers.
  • HYPE SHOP: Unlock permanent bonuses: sponsor deals, viral editing, a production crew, a loyal fanbase and gift fever.

FEATURES

  • Offline income: no internet needed, and your channel keeps producing for up to 8 hours while the app is closed.
  • 10 studios: from a bedroom to a global media empire, then into orbit and the metaverse.
  • Rivals, team and gear models: buy shares in rival channels, upgrade 8 permanent team cards and unlock 3 new models for each piece of gear.
  • 18 milestone quests, 21 achievements, daily quests, a STRATEGY tree, life events and login streak rewards.
  • Profile, avatars and frames; weekly, global and friends leaderboards; a shareable stats card.
  • A tutorial for your first minute, skippable at any time.
  • Save transfer: export your progress as text and import it on a new device.
  • 8 languages: English, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), German, French, Indonesian, Italian.
  • Rewarded ads only, never forced. Purchases are optional cosmetics (avatars, frames, outfits); no sign-up needed.
  • Small download, portrait screen, one-handed play.

If you enjoy offline idle games and tycoon or vlogger games, this influencer game keeps your channel growing even when you put the phone down. Build your channel, grow your fanbase, start your first livestream and watch the subscribers roll in.

Idle Tok is not a real social media app; it connects to no account, uploads no videos anywhere and needs no e-mail; only the nickname you pick (if any) appears on leaderboards.

Formerly known as Idle Tok: Viral Tycoon.
