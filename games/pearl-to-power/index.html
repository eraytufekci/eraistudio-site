About the game

Pearl to Power is a city builder and strategy game set on a fictional Gulf coast. Start with a small pearl-diving village and lead it through the Pearl, Oil and Future Eras. Nothing is lost when a new era begins: buildings evolve, and the levels you have earned stay with you.

BUILD YOUR GULF TOWN

Raise the Majlis, dig the falaj, plant date groves and open the souq. Add wind tower houses, a harbor, a shipyard and a pearl atelier. Run your production chains like a tycoon: oysters become pearls and jewelry, dates become date syrup, and mudbrick, beams and rope raise new buildings. Buildings are linked: most upgrades need their supplier to keep up, so plan ahead. Fill orders on the order board to earn coins. Piers and groves keep producing while you are away, up to their storage limit.

SAIL THE TRADE ROUTES

Send jalbuts, sambuks and booms to distant ports, and later tankers and container ships. Watch your ships cross the region map and return with timber and spices.

FROM PEARLS TO OIL

Discover oil and watch your town change. Oil wells, a refinery, a pipeline and a tank farm reshape the skyline. The oil price moves every day and tomorrow's range is shown in advance, so you decide when to sell, when to store and how much to put into your Wealth Fund for tourism, technology and infrastructure. An oil tycoon game, played one decision at a time.

A CITY OF THE FUTURE

Open an airport and hotels, welcome visitors, build solar farms, office towers, a marina and a data center, and bid for tourism islands on the region map. When your Majlis reaches level 30, take on grand projects: an artificial island, an observation tower, a sea bridge, a desert garden and an astronomy center.

A STORY, CHAPTER BY CHAPTER

When artificial pearls end the old trade, your town must find another way. Characters such as a captain, a merchant and an engineer guide you through the story; your advisors work in their own buildings and grow with levels and equipment. In Research, every step lets you choose one of two paths.

RIVALRY WITHOUT ARMIES

There are no armies here: competition is economic. Join an alliance that grows from 30 up to 50 members, raise its flag and chat with your alliance or your whole region in your own words or with quick messages; messages in another language can be translated on your phone. Climb the weekly leagues from Sand to Falcon. Twice a week, alliance wars decide who holds pearl banks, trade routes and oil fields. Bid in daily auctions for pearl beds, port rights and oil fields. Try voyage raids, souq raids and takeovers against rival towns, and protect your own with shields, the watchtower, the pearl vault and help from your alliance.

TRADITIONAL MINI GAMES

Gather oysters in the pearl diving game before your breath runs out, race dhows with the wind, call your falcon in falconry and ride in horse and camel races. Mini games are won with skill: no betting, no luck.

EVENTS ALL YEAR

Most days bring three events: the Noon race, the Evening task with your alliance and the Merchant fleet at night. Ramadan, Eid and national days are big celebrations, each with its own Celebration shop. Seasons last four weeks and end with rewards and badges, and the Skyline race celebrates the most impressive cities. Your town lights up in the evening, on your local time.

FAIR BY DESIGN

Purchases are optional and save time; gems can also be earned by playing. Ads play only when you tap to watch for a reward, such as faster building. There are no forced ads. Every pack shows exactly what it contains. There are no loot boxes and no random paid rewards, and oil cannot be bought or sold with gems.

Pearl to Power brings city building, tycoon economy and alliance strategy together on the Gulf coast: a town builder and city building simulator with a story. Choose a male or female avatar, name your town and build it your way.

Play in English, Arabic or Turkish, with adjustable text size. An internet connection is required.
