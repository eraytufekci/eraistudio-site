About the game

All aboard, Boss! Tekir Express is a cozy family board game of dice and trains, set across all 81 provinces of Türkiye. Roll the dice, ride your train from city to city, be the first to reach the destination on Mr. Tekir's station sign and grab the Arrival Bonus.

🏪 Local businesses: from kebab shops to tea factories, from hazelnut co-ops to baklava workshops, 219 businesses are waiting for you. Buy every business in a city to take it over and double your profits there. A fun business board game where luck meets strategy.

🧞 The Debt Genie: it jumps on the train furthest from the target, sends bills and buys useless cards. Pass through another player's stop to hand it over! Carry it too long and the Genie Sultan may come…

🃏 40 original cards: High-Speed Train Ticket, Lobster Step, Evil Eye Bead, Snowstorm, Charmed Jar and more.

📰 Seasons and events: "Breaking News!" from Reporter Stork; beach tourism in summer, snow in the East in winter.

🎲 Fair dice: every roll is set by the seed at the start of the game, and computer players cannot see the dice. The Dice History is always available.

🎯 Scenarios and the Boss's Notebook: 12 Easy, Medium and Hard scenarios, 28 achievements and an album of 219 businesses.

👨‍👩‍👧 A 2–4 player board game: pass and play with family or friends, and AI opponents (Apprentice, Journeyman, Master) take any empty seats. Quick Match (about half an hour) or Classic games of 3 to 50 years, and the Weekly Challenge. Kids mode, larger text, high contrast and color-blind support.

🌐 Online Friend Match: a board game with friends far away! Send them an invite code, and everyone plays on their own phone. When your turn comes, you make your move; a game can last for days. Online matches are not open to all ages.

📴 An offline board game too: no internet and no account needed to play solo or together on one phone.

Free to download: Quick Match, the 3-year Classic game, Apprentice and Journeyman rivals, up to 2 players on the same device, the Easy scenarios, the Weekly Challenge and joining friend matches are always free. Even without the Full Version, you can fill the table up to 4 players with AI opponents. A one-time Full Version unlocks 5-, 10-, 20- and 50-year Classic games, Master rivals, 3–4 players on the same device, the Medium and Hard scenarios and creating online friend matches. No ads. No subscription.
